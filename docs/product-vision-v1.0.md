# Product Vision Document v1.0

# Working Title

**IdeaOS – An AI Cognitive Operating System for Builders**

---

# Vision Statement

IdeaOS is an AI-powered cognitive operating system that helps creators, researchers, founders, engineers, and knowledge workers transform thousands of ideas into deliberate, high-impact decisions.

Rather than managing tasks, IdeaOS manages the evolution of ideas, protects long-term missions, and prevents unconscious project drift.

Its purpose is not to increase productivity.

Its purpose is to improve decision quality.

---

# The Problem

Creative people rarely fail because they lack ideas.

They fail because every new idea feels equally important.

Over time this creates:

* Endless project switching
* Recursive infrastructure building
* Scope explosion
* Analysis paralysis
* Forgotten ideas
* Unfinished products

Current productivity software manages tasks.

Knowledge management software manages information.

Project management software manages work.

None of them manage the **evolution of ideas and the decisions surrounding them.**

---

# Product Philosophy

Every idea deserves to be remembered.

Not every idea deserves to be built today.

The purpose of AI is not to tell people what to do.

The purpose of AI is to help people understand:

* where ideas came from,
* why they appeared,
* what they cost,
* what they enable,
* and when they should become reality.

---

# Mission

Help people make conscious commitments instead of emotional commitments.

---

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Every idea has a history.

Ideas do not appear from nowhere.

Every idea should be connected to its ancestors.

---

## Principle 2

Every decision has an opportunity cost.

Building one idea delays another.

The AI should make those costs visible.

---

## Principle 3

Nothing is forgotten.

Ideas are never discarded.

They are preserved until evidence suggests they should be activated or archived.

---

## Principle 4

The AI never makes the decision.

The user remains the decision maker.

The AI provides evidence, reasoning and consequences.

---

## Principle 5

Ideas should be activated by evidence rather than excitement.

## One recommendation before you write any code

I would add one final principle that isn't about the product—it's about **building the product itself**.

> **IdeaOS must use its own philosophy during development.**

Every feature request for IdeaOS should pass through the same pipeline:

1. Is this feature derived from an existing feature or is it genuinely new?
2. Does it directly advance the current milestone?
3. What is its activation condition?
4. What is its opportunity cost?
5. Should it be **Build Now**, **Schedule**, **Incubate**, or **Archive**?

If the product can successfully guide its own evolution without collapsing under feature creep, it will have validated its core promise before its first public release. That's a powerful way to ensure the vision stays coherent as the project grows.
